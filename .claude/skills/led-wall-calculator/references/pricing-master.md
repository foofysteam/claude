## MS Frame Support

The structural mild steel frame behind the LED panels. Always required.

| Type | Rate | When to Use |
|------|------|-------------|
| **Standard MS frame** | ₹250/sqft | Default for all installations — covers the LED panel mounting area |
| **Outdoor small (flat)** | ₹20,000 flat | For small outdoor screens (≤50 sqft) where a simple frame suffices |
| **Outdoor large (per sqft)** | ₹185-250/sqft | For large outdoor installations — varies by height and wind load |

**The default is ₹250/sqft for the area of the LED panels.** This is the standard structural support cost.
