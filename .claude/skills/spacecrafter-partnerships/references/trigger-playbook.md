# Trigger playbook — South India clients

Retail, QSR, real estate, hospitals, clinics across Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi and Coimbatore.

---

## The principle

A static list of every hospital in Karnataka is worth very little. Nobody buys signage because an email arrived. They buy because **a building is opening and the signage is not done yet.**

So prospect on events, not on categories. The question is never "who could need signage" but "who has a live project that will need signage in the next two to six months."

---

## The timing window

**Signage is specified during fit-out, roughly two to four months before opening.**

- Too early — structure going up, no interiors, no budget line allocated yet, nobody to talk to
- **The window** — structure complete, interiors underway, fit-out contractors appointed, brand rolling out
- Too late — opening announced publicly, signage already quoted and probably awarded

The practical signal for the window opening: **interior fit-out has started, and the opening date has not been announced.** Once a specific opening date is public, assume you are late.

## Triggers by sector

### Hospitals and clinics

Highest signage spend per project of any sector. Wayfinding is not decoration in a hospital, it is a safety and throughput requirement, and it is regulated.

| Trigger | Why it matters | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| New block, wing or bed expansion announced | Wayfinding for a new block has to be designed from scratch | Hospital press releases, local business press, health ministry announcements |
| New branch in a new city or suburb | Full signage package, exterior through wayfinding | Chain hospital newsrooms, LinkedIn company posts |
| **NABH accreditation drive or renewal** | **NABH requires compliant signage and wayfinding. This is a regulatory forcing function, not a preference.** | NABH accredited/applicant hospital lists |
| Major equipment install (MR-Linac, cath lab, new ICU) | Implies a new wing, which implies wayfinding | Medical trade press, equipment vendor announcements |
| Rebrand or acquisition by a larger chain | Every sign in the building gets replaced | Business press, M&A news |

**NABH is the strongest single trigger in this playbook.** A hospital pursuing or renewing accreditation has a documented obligation, a deadline, and a budget.

### Retail

| Trigger | Why it matters | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| D2C brand raising a round and going offline | First physical stores, no existing signage vendor relationship | Entrackr, Inc42, VCCircle, YourStory |
| New mall pre-leasing or launch | Every incoming tenant needs storefront signage | Mall developer announcements, retail trade press |
| Chain announcing expansion targets | Repeat volume across many sites | Company investor updates, press |
| Format change or store refresh programme | Refits the whole estate | Retail trade press |
| Hiring store managers in a new city | Reliable early signal of stores opening | LinkedIn job posts, Naukri |

**The hiring signal is underused.** A retail chain posting store manager roles in a city it does not yet operate in is opening there, usually before any press release.

### QSR

| Trigger | Why it matters | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| Master franchise for India or a South India region awarded | Whole new chain, whole new signage standard | Restaurant and franchise trade press |
| Cloud kitchen moving to dine-in | First real storefront | Food business press, brand social |
| New outlet announcements | Repeat, predictable volume | Brand social accounts, franchise portals |
| International QSR entering India | New market entry, no supplier relationships | Business press |

QSR is the best sector for **repeat** work. One approved signage standard gets rebuilt at every new outlet.

### Real estate

| Trigger | Why it matters | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| **RERA registration for a new project** | **Public, dated, searchable, and precedes marketing. The single most reliable structured source in this playbook.** | Karnataka RERA, TN RERA, Telangana RERA portals |
| Pre-launch marketing beginning | Site branding, hoardings, sales gallery, show flat | Developer social and website |
| Occupancy certificate received | Fit-out phase, permanent signage and wayfinding | RERA portals, developer announcements |
| New commercial tower or business park announced | Tenant signage, wayfinding, facade identity | Commercial property press |
| Co-working operator taking new floors | Interior branding and wayfinding per site | Operator announcements, LinkedIn |

**Start with the RERA portals.** Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Telangana all publish registered projects with dates and developer details. It is structured, official and free, and it is the closest thing to a prospect database that actually exists for this sector.

## Sources worth checking regularly

**Structured and official**
- Karnataka RERA, TN RERA, Telangana RERA project registers
- NABH accredited and applicant hospital lists
- Municipal signage and hoarding permit filings where published

**News and funding**
- Entrackr, Inc42, VCCircle, YourStory — funding rounds, especially D2C going offline
- Local business press: Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi editions
- Retail, restaurant and healthcare trade publications

**Company-published**
- LinkedIn company pages — expansion posts and job listings
- Brand social accounts — "coming soon" posts are a direct fit-out signal
- Press and newsroom pages

## Qualifying before drafting

Do not draft until all four hold:

1. **The project is real and current** — a dated source from the last three months, not an old announcement
2. **The signage is plausibly unspecified** — fit-out underway, opening date not yet public
3. **There is a contactable role** — a projects, facilities or expansion person, named
4. **The address is verified** — read off a real page. If not verifiable, route to the published corporate or projects inbox and ask for the person by name in the body.

Rule 4 is where the four bounces came from. See `verification-rules.md`.

## What the client email must do

Lead with the project, not the company profile. The first line proves you noticed something specific and current. Everything after that earns the right to a reply.

Full template and worked examples in `pitch-library.md` under **Variant E**.

Three things to avoid:

- **Do not open with your own credentials.** They do not care yet.
- **Do not list your full service range.** Name the two or three things their specific project needs.
- **Do not guess the budget or the timeline.** You do not know, and being wrong in the first email ends it.
