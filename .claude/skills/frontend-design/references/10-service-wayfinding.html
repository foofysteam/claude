01 — The Challenge

Lost Visitors Cost More Than You Think

Navigation Frustration

Visitors wandering corridors, patients missing appointments, employees wasting time giving directions — a poorly signed facility creates daily friction that compounds into real operational cost.

No Signage Strategy

Signs added reactively over the years — different fonts, different materials, different mounting heights. The result is visual chaos that makes navigation harder, not easier.

Accessibility Failures

Missing tactile signs, non-compliant room markers, no multilingual support — your facility isn't accessible to everyone, and you may be falling short of regulatory requirements.

Growth Without Updates

New wings, renamed departments, relocated offices — but the signage hasn't kept up. Outdated directories and wrong room numbers create confusion and erode trust.

Wayfinding Systems Installed

Wayfinding isn't about signs — it's about designing intuitive journeys. Every decision point, every corridor, every elevator landing needs to answer one question: where do I go next?

We design wayfinding as a complete system — from traffic flow analysis and sign placement planning through modular sign design, fabrication, and installation. Our systems are built for growth — add floors, rename departments, relocate offices without replacing the entire system.

02 — Our Process

From Facility Audit to Seamless Navigation

1
Facility Audit

Complete walkthrough mapping every decision point, intersection, elevator, stairwell, and entry/exit. We document visitor flows, pain points, and existing signage — creating a wayfinding gap analysis.

2
System Design

Sign family design — directories, blade signs, room markers, floor IDs, parking signs, and emergency signs — all in a unified visual language with your brand identity and colour coding.

3
Placement Planning

Strategic sign placement at every decision point. We create a master sign schedule — sign type, location, content, mounting method, and height for every single sign in the system.

4
Fabrication

Modular sign systems with changeable inserts. Powder-coated aluminium frames, acrylic panels, vinyl lettering, tactile Braille markers, and LED illumination — all fabricated in-house.

5
Installation & Training

Professional installation with level precision. We train your facilities team on how to update sign inserts, replace panels, and maintain the system as your organisation evolves.
