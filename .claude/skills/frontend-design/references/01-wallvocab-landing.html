Walls For India

Walls that get noticed.

WallVocab is the custom wall design service from SpaceCrafter Studio in Bangalore. We work with homeowners, architects, interior designers, and brand managers across India who want walls that aren't generic — walls that hold meaning, mood, and craft. From a single bedroom feature wall to entire office floors, every project is custom-curated, custom-fabricated, and custom-installed.

What kind of walls does WallVocab design?

Interior walls in homes, offices, hospitality, retail, and clinics. Single accent walls, gallery compositions, brand walls, and full architectural finishes.

Where is WallVocab based?

Our studio and 10,000 sq ft fabrication facility is in Bangalore, India. We deliver and install across India.

How is WallVocab different from buying art online?

Online stores sell pieces. WallVocab designs the whole wall — the art, the frame, the finish, and the install — so the result fits the room you actually have.
