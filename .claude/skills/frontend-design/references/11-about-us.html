Our Journey

From Aarkay Signs to SpaceCrafter Studio

Our story began in 1988, when Aarkay Signs opened as a precision signage workshop in Bengaluru. What started with neon lights and hand-painted boards soon grew into a trusted name for quality signages across India.

By 2010, we had evolved into a creative studio offering wall graphics, architectural branding, and wayfinding solutions. In 2015, we stepped up as a private limited company, embracing new technologies and bigger ideas.

Today, as SpaceCrafter Studio, we combine spatial design with advanced signage and LED solutions to craft experiences that inspire, engage, and elevate every environment we touch. Strategy to installation, all under one roof.

Milestones

Three Decades of Building Brand Environments

1988

Aarkay Signs founded in Bengaluru as a precision signage workshop, specialising in neon lights and hand-painted boards.

1995

Expanded into illuminated signages and large-format neon installations for commercial clients across Karnataka.

2005

First hospital wayfinding project — entering the healthcare sector and pioneering multi-level navigation systems.

2010

Evolved into a creative studio offering wall graphics, murals, architectural branding, and environmental design.

2015

Became a private limited company. Invested in LED technology, digital signage, and CNC fabrication capabilities.

2020

Rebranded as SpaceCrafter Studio — full-service spatial branding partner offering 7 integrated service verticals.

2024

Serving 9,000+ clients across 50+ Indian cities with ISO-certified in-house manufacturing and pan-India installation teams.
