02 — Our Process

From Brief to Brushstroke

1
Discovery & Brief

We study your brand, space dimensions, lighting conditions, foot traffic patterns, and the story you want to tell — whether it's brand heritage, company values, or pure artistic expression.

2
Concept & Visualisation

Our designers create multiple concept options with AI-powered previews showing exactly how the mural will look on your actual wall — including scale, perspective, and lighting effects.

3
Surface Preparation

Professional wall treatment — cleaning, priming, crack repair, and texture preparation to ensure the mural surface is perfect for long-lasting paint adhesion and colour vibrancy.

4
Painting & Execution

Our trained artists hand-paint using high-grade exterior/interior paints with UV protection. Large murals use a combination of projection mapping, stenciling, and freehand techniques.

5
Protection & Handover

Anti-graffiti and UV-resistant clear coats protect your investment for 8-10 years. We provide care guides, touch-up kits, and maintenance support.

Mural & Custom Art FAQs

  • We paint on plastered walls, concrete, brick, metal, wood, and glass. Each surface requires specific preparation — we handle all of it. For textured or damaged walls, we include surface treatment in our scope.

  • Indoor murals with our protective coating last 8-10 years with minimal fading. Exterior murals last 5-7 years depending on weather exposure. We use UV-resistant paints and anti-graffiti clear coats for maximum longevity.
