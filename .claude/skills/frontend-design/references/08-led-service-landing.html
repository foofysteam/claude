03 — Solutions

LED Solutions for Every Environment

Indoor Video Walls

Seamless high-resolution displays for lobbies, conference rooms, control centres, and retail environments. Fine pixel pitch (P1.5–P2.5) for close viewing.

Outdoor LED Displays

Weather-resistant, high-brightness panels for building facades, billboards, and stadium screens. IP65-rated with 6,000+ nits for direct sunlight.

Transparent LED Screens

Glass-mounted LED mesh that displays content while maintaining 60-85% transparency. Perfect for storefronts, showrooms, and glass facades.

LED Channel Letters

Illuminated 3D brand lettering with front-lit, back-lit, or halo-lit options. Fabricated in-house with energy-efficient LED modules.

Digital Menu & Ticker Boards

Scrolling tickers, digital menu boards, and information displays for restaurants, transit hubs, trading floors, and corporate lobbies.

  • Costs vary based on pixel pitch, panel size, indoor/outdoor rating, and installation complexity. Indoor video walls typically start from ₹15,000-25,000 per sq ft installed. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your specific requirements and site conditions.
