## 1. Structural Steel Grades

### IS 2062:2011 — Hot Rolled Structural Steel

| Grade | Designation | Yield Strength (MPa) | UTS (MPa) | Elongation (%) | Common Use |
|-------|------------|---------------------|----------|---------------|-----------|
| E250 A | Fe 410WA | 250 | 410 | 23 | General structural — most pylon work |
| E250 B | Fe 410WB | 250 | 410 | 23 | Improved weldability |
| E250 C | Fe 410WC | 250 | 410 | 23 | Impact tested — for cold regions |
| E350 A | Fe 490A | 350 | 490 | 22 | Higher strength — tall pylons, long brackets |

## 2. Hollow Section Selection Guide

### When to Use Which Section

| Application | Recommended Section | Why |
|-------------|-------------------|-----|
| Pylon main column (aesthetic important) | CHS | Smooth appearance, best wind profile (lowest Cd) |
| Pylon main column (economy) | SHS or RHS | Easy to fabricate, flat faces for mounting |
| Pylon bracing members | SHS or angle | Standard sizes readily available |
| Sign cabinet main frame | RHS | Strong axis for bending, flat for panel mounting |
| Sign cabinet secondary frame | SHS 40×40 to 50×50 | LED panel support rails |
| Cantilever bracket arm | RHS (strong axis into wind) | Maximum bending resistance |
| Lattice tower main chords | CHS or angle | Traditional, well-understood connections |
| Lattice tower bracing | CHS or flat bar | Compact connections |

### Commonly Stocked Sizes in India

**CHS readily available:**
- 48.3mm, 60.3mm, 76.1mm, 88.9mm, 114.3mm, 139.7mm, 168.3mm, 219.1mm, 273.0mm, 323.9mm OD
- Thicknesses: 2.6mm, 3.2mm, 4.0mm, 4.5mm, 5.0mm, 6.3mm, 8.0mm

**SHS readily available:**
- 25×25, 30×30, 40×40, 50×50, 60×60, 75×75, 80×80, 100×100, 120×120, 150×150, 200×200mm
- Thicknesses: 2.0mm, 2.6mm, 3.0mm, 3.2mm, 4.0mm, 5.0mm, 6.3mm, 8.0mm

**RHS readily available:**
- 50×25, 60×40, 75×50, 80×40, 100×50, 100×60, 120×60, 150×75, 150×100, 200×100, 250×150mm
- Thicknesses: 2.0mm, 2.6mm, 3.0mm, 3.2mm, 4.0mm, 5.0mm, 6.3mm

## 4. Fastener Specifications

### Bolt Grades Available in India

| Property Class | Proof Stress (MPa) | UTS (MPa) | Yield (MPa) | Use |
|---------------|-------------------|----------|------------|-----|
| 4.6 | 225 | 400 | 240 | General non-structural, light connections |
| 4.8 | 310 | 420 | 340 | Semi-structural |
| 8.8 | 580 | 800 | 640 | Structural connections — primary choice for signage |
| 10.9 | 830 | 1040 | 940 | High-strength — special applications |
| 12.9 | 970 | 1220 | 1100 | Very high-strength — rarely used in signage |

**Standard for signage structural connections: Grade 8.8**

### Anchor Systems

**Mechanical Expansion Anchors:**
- Suitable for uncracked concrete only
- Quick installation
- Lower capacity than chemical anchors
- Use for non-critical secondary connections

**Chemical Anchors (Epoxy/Vinylester Resin):**
- Primary choice for structural sign mounting
- Higher capacity than mechanical
- Works in cracked and uncracked concrete
- Install temperature: 5°C to 40°C
- Cure time: 15 minutes (epoxy, 20°C) to 24 hours (cold weather)
- Brands commonly available in India: Hilti, Fischer, Rawlplug, Sika

**Through-Bolts:**
- When access to both sides of the mounting surface is available
- Highest reliability — no dependency on concrete condition
- Preferred for life-safety applications

## 6. Corrosion Protection

### Hot-Dip Galvanising (IS 2629:1985 / IS 4759)

| Steel Thickness | Minimum Zinc Coating (g/m²) | Minimum Coating Thickness (μm) |
|----------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| < 1.5mm | 325 | 45 |
| 1.5mm to 3.0mm | 395 | 55 |
| 3.0mm to 5.0mm | 460 | 63 |
| > 5.0mm | 610 | 85 |

**Expected service life of galvanising:**
- Rural environment: 50+ years
- Urban/suburban: 25-40 years
- Industrial: 15-25 years
- Marine/coastal: 10-20 years

### Paint Systems for Signage Structures

**System A — Economy (interior or sheltered):**
1. Wire brush / power tool clean to St 3
2. Zinc phosphate primer: 50μm
3. Alkyd topcoat: 50μm
- Total DFT: 100μm, Life expectancy: 5-8 years

**System B — Standard (general exterior):**
1. Blast clean to SA 2.5
2. Zinc-rich primer: 75μm
3. Epoxy MIO intermediate: 125μm
4. Polyurethane topcoat: 50μm
- Total DFT: 250μm, Life expectancy: 15-20 years

**System C — Heavy Duty (coastal/industrial):**
1. Blast clean to SA 2.5
2. Zinc-rich primer (inorganic): 75μm
3. High-build epoxy: 200μm
4. Polyurethane topcoat: 75μm
- Total DFT: 350μm, Life expectancy: 20-25 years

**System D — Duplex (galvanise + paint):**
1. Hot-dip galvanise per IS 2629
2. Sweep blast to roughen surface
3. Epoxy primer: 50μm
4. Polyurethane topcoat: 50μm
- Total DFT: galv + 100μm, Life expectancy: 30+ years

**Default for outdoor signage structures: System B or D**

## 9. LED Panel Structural Integration

### P-Value LED Panel Specifications (Common Outdoor)

| Panel Type | Pixel Pitch | Panel Size (mm) | Weight (kg) | Power (W) | Use |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|-------------|-----------|-----|
| P4 | 4mm | 256×128 | 1.2 | 40 | Close viewing distance |
| P5 | 5mm | 320×160 | 1.5 | 45 | Standard retail |
| P6 | 6mm | 192×192 | 1.8 | 55 | Medium distance |
| P8 | 8mm | 256×128 | 2.0 | 55 | Typical pylon displays |
| P10 | 10mm | 320×160 | 2.5 | 65 | Highway/road pylons |
| P16 | 16mm | 256×256 | 4.0 | 80 | Large distance viewing |

### Structural Loads from LED Panels

```
Dead load per m² of LED display:
  P4-P6: 30-35 kg/m²
  P8-P10: 35-45 kg/m²
  P16: 45-55 kg/m²

  Add for mounting hardware: +5 kg/m²
  Add for cables, power supplies: +3 kg/m²
  Total dead load estimate: Panel weight + 8 kg/m²
```

### Mounting Methods

**Rear-access module system (preferred for maintenance):**
- LED modules slot in from the rear
- Cabinet depth: minimum panel depth + 100mm for access + 100mm for wiring
- Structural frame: rails at module pitch spacing

**Front-access magnetic system:**
- LED modules attach with magnets from front
- Cabinet depth: can be reduced (panel depth + 50mm)
- Structural frame: flat backing plate with magnet receivers

### Thermal Management

LED panels generate significant heat. The cabinet structure must account for:
- Ventilation openings: minimum 10% of cabinet face area
- Fan mounting provisions (for enclosed cabinets)
- Ensure ventilation doesn't compromise IP rating — use louvred vents with rain shields
